# Meal Prep Starter System

Meal prep should make your week easier, not more complicated.

## Weekly rhythm

1. Pick meals on Friday or Saturday
2. Shop once with a list
3. Prep in one focused session
4. Store portions clearly and label dates

## Prep priorities

- Cook proteins in batches
- Prepare one grain or starch base
- Chop vegetables for quick assembly
- Keep sauces separate for freshness

## Keep flexibility

Build components you can mix and match, rather than fully fixed meals.
