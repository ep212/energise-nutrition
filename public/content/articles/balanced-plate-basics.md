# Balanced Plate Basics

A balanced plate is a practical visual tool for everyday meals.

## A simple formula

- Half plate: vegetables and fruit
- Quarter plate: protein source
- Quarter plate: high-fiber carbohydrate
- Add healthy fats in small portions

## Why this works

This structure improves satiety, stabilizes energy, and supports nutrient variety.

## Easy examples

- Grilled chicken, quinoa, mixed greens, olive oil dressing
- Lentil curry, brown rice, roasted vegetables
- Greek yogurt bowl with berries, oats, and nuts
