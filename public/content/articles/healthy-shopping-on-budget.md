# Healthy Shopping On A Budget

Eating well on a budget starts with a reliable plan.

## Before you shop

- Plan 3 to 4 core meals for the week
- Build a focused list around those meals
- Check your pantry first to avoid duplicates

## Smart budget moves

- Choose frozen produce when fresh is expensive
- Buy staple proteins in larger packs and portion at home
- Compare unit prices, not just shelf prices

## Keep it realistic

Use 1 to 2 repeatable breakfasts and lunches to simplify decisions.
