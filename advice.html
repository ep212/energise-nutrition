Vitamins are essential for health and normal growth and development. Unlike cabrs, proteins and fats, they don't directly provide us with energy, although they are involved in linking and regulating the various systems in our bodies that extract energy from food. Vitamins are a micronutrient, micro because they are needed in smaller amounts than other nutrients, typically milli and microgram quantities.
