Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates are our bodies primary source of energy. According to UK government guidelines, 50-55% of the energy we consume should come from carbohydrates. This means that 50-55% of our calorie intake should be from carbohydrates. Of this, only 5% should come from added sugars. Seen as such a large bulk of our diet should consist of carbohydrates, it's important to understand what they do, the differences between different sources, and how to make them work for you and fit your goals. Please be aware that the percentages mentioned are 'guidelines', everybody is different and people have different needs, they're not a one size fits all figure.
